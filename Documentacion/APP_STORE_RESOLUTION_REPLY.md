The “Redeem access code” feature lets a user enter a share code generated by another CraftQuest user (a tutor or classmate) for a quiz they created. Redeeming grants access to practice that specific user-created quiz and adds it to “Shared quizzes”.

It does NOT unlock:
• Subscriptions (Pro / Tutor)
• AI credit packs
• Prep+ catalog content
• Class membership (teachers invite students by email, not redeem codes)

Paid plans and credits require a separate In-App Purchase.
